Welcome to the ASU Course Management System (CMS)

The ASU CMS is a comprehensive digital platform designed to simplify and enhance the learning experience for students and faculty. From managing assignments to tracking course progress, our CMS provides an all-in-one solution to keep you organized and engaged throughout your academic journey.

Whether you’re a student submitting coursework, an instructor uploading materials, or an administrator managing classes, the ASU CMS ensures a seamless and secure experience across all devices.

Key Features:

  • 📘 Easy course and assignment management
  • 🔒 Secure user authentication and access control
  • 📊 Real-time performance tracking and analytics
  • 💬 Communication tools for faculty and students
  • ☁️ Cloud-based storage for submissions and resources
