Help & Documentation

Students
  • Browse courses from Courses.
  • Enrollments are managed by your lecturer/admin.
  • Submit assignments from the course page; view your results in My Submissions.
Lecturers
  • Use the Admin Console to manage your courses and assignments.
  • Enroll or remove students via Courses → Enrollments.
  • Grade from Assignments → Submissions; students will see grades instantly.
Admins
  • Create courses and users, assign lecturers to courses.
  • Have full access to all data and settings.
